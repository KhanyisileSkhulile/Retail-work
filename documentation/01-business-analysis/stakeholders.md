# Retail-X-AI Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Introduction

Retail-X-AI is an AI-powered inventory demand forecasting and smart restocking system designed for local spaza shops and small grocery stores in South African townships.

The system will help shop owners manage products, inventory and sales while providing demand forecasts, stockout warnings, restocking recommendations, slow-moving product information and expiry alerts.

The stakeholder analysis identifies the people and groups who interact with the system, are affected by the system, or have an interest in its successful implementation.

---

## 2. Primary Stakeholders

### 2.1 Shop Owner

**Role:** Primary business user and decision maker.

**Responsibilities:**

• Manage the shop's products and inventory.

• Monitor sales.

• Review demand forecasts.

• Review stockout alerts.

• Review restocking recommendations.

• Monitor slow-moving products.

• Monitor expiry warnings.

• Use the AI assistant to ask questions about business data.

**Needs:**

• Clear and understandable information.

• Accurate inventory information.

• Useful sales information.

• Demand forecasts.

• Restocking recommendations.

• Alerts for important inventory problems.

• A simple dashboard.

**Interest:** High

**Influence:** High

---

### 2.2 Shop Manager

**Role:** Operational user who assists with the daily management of the shop.

**Responsibilities:**

• Record sales.

• Update inventory.

• Manage product information.

• Monitor stock levels.

• Review alerts.

• Assist the shop owner with daily operations.

**Needs:**

• Easy access to inventory information.

• Simple sales recording.

• Accurate stock information.

• Clear alerts.

• Reliable system functionality.

**Interest:** High

**Influence:** High

---

### 2.3 Shop Employees

**Role:** Operational users who may record sales and assist with inventory activities.

**Responsibilities:**

• Record customer sales.

• Update stock information where authorised.

• Assist with product management.

• Report inventory problems.

**Needs:**

• Easy to use interfaces.

• Simple data entry.

• Clear instructions.

• Appropriate access permissions.

**Interest:** Medium

**Influence:** Medium

---

### 2.4 Customers

**Role:** Indirect stakeholders who purchase products from the shop.

Customers do not necessarily interact directly with Retail-X-AI, but they are affected by the quality of inventory management.

**Needs:**

• Products should be available when required.

• Reduced occurrence of stockouts.

• Efficient service.

• Appropriate product availability.

**Interest:** Medium

**Influence:** Low

---

### 2.5 Suppliers

**Role:** External stakeholders who provide products to the shop.

**Responsibilities:**

• Supply products.

• Provide product availability information where applicable.

• Support the shop's replenishment process.

**Needs:**

• Clear purchasing information.

• Accurate product and order information.

• Appropriate communication with the shop.

**Interest:** Medium

**Influence:** Medium

---

### 2.6 System Administrator

**Role:** Technical stakeholder responsible for administration and maintenance of the system.

**Responsibilities:**

• Manage user accounts.

• Maintain system access.

• Support system configuration.

• Monitor system operation.

• Assist with technical problems.

• Support security and maintenance activities.

**Needs:**

• Administrative controls.

• Secure authentication.

• User management.

• System monitoring.

• Error information.

**Interest:** High

**Influence:** High

## 5. Stakeholder Requirements

The stakeholder analysis indicates that Retail-X-AI should provide:

1. Simple product and inventory management.
2. Sales recording and monitoring.
3. Clear inventory visibility.
4. Demand forecasting.
5. Stockout warnings.
6. Smart restocking recommendations.
7. Slow-moving product identification.
8. Expiry warnings.
9. A useful dashboard.
10. An AI assistant for business data queries.
11. Secure user access.
12. Appropriate access permissions.
13. Reliable and understandable information.

---

## 6. Stakeholder Priorities

The primary focus of the system should be the operational needs of the shop owner and shop manager because they are the main users responsible for inventory and purchasing decisions.

Employees require simple interfaces for operational activities such as sales and inventory updates.

Customers are indirect stakeholders because the system is intended to improve product availability and reduce stockouts.

Suppliers may benefit from improved purchasing and replenishment decisions.

The system administrator requires appropriate technical and security controls to maintain the system.

The project team requires clear requirements, responsibilities, testing procedures and project controls to successfully deliver the system.

---

## 7. Stakeholder Analysis Conclusion

The stakeholder analysis shows that Retail-X-AI must focus on providing accurate, understandable and useful inventory information to the shop owner and other authorised users.

The system must support the main business activities of managing products, recording sales, monitoring inventory, forecasting demand and making restocking decisions.

The identified stakeholder needs will be used as input for the next stages of the project, including requirements analysis, use case development, system design and database design.

# Business Objectives and Success Criteria

## 1. Introduction

Retail-X-AI is intended to help small retail businesses, particularly local spaza shops, improve inventory management and make better purchasing decisions.

The system will use sales and stock information to provide demand forecasts, reorder recommendations, alerts and an AI chatbot. The following business objectives and success criteria define what the project aims to achieve and how its success will be measured.

---

## 2. Business Objectives

### BO-01 — Reduce Stockouts

Retail-X-AI aims to reduce stockouts of high-demand products by at least 30% within three months of adoption.

This will help shop owners maintain the availability of products that customers frequently purchase and reduce lost sales opportunities.

### BO-02 — Reduce Inventory Holding Costs and Expiry Losses

The system aims to decrease inventory holding costs and product expiry losses by between 20% and 25%.

This will be supported through improved demand forecasting, stock monitoring, reorder recommendations and identification of slow-moving or near-expiry products.

### BO-03 — Provide Demand Forecasts

Retail-X-AI aims to provide accurate weekly and monthly demand forecasts for the top 50–100 products.

These forecasts will help shop owners plan purchasing decisions based on expected demand.

### BO-04 — Enable Natural-Language Queries

The system aims to allow shop owners to query stock and sales information using natural language through an AI chatbot.

This will allow users to obtain useful information without needing advanced technical or data-analysis skills.

### BO-05 — Provide a Simple Visual Dashboard

Retail-X-AI aims to provide a simple visual dashboard displaying:

- Current stock levels.
- Predicted demand.
- Reorder recommendations.
- Stock alerts.
- Other relevant inventory information.

The dashboard should allow shop owners to understand their shop's current situation quickly and support informed decision-making.

---

## 3. Business Success Criteria

The success of Retail-X-AI will be measured using the following criteria.

### SC-01 — Reorder Recommendation Adoption

At least 70% of system-generated reorder recommendations should be accepted and actioned by the shop owner.

### SC-02 — Reduction in Expired Stock and Lost Sales

A measurable reduction in expired stock and lost sales incidents should be achieved within the first quarter of system use.

### SC-03 — Positive User Feedback

Pilot shop owners should provide positive feedback regarding the ease of use and usefulness of the AI chatbot and visual dashboard.

### SC-04 — System Operational Performance

The system should remain operational on modest hardware, such as a standard laptop or mid-range smartphone.

## 5. Measurement Approach

The project team will use measurable indicators to determine whether the business objectives are being achieved.

### Stockout Reduction

Stockout incidents before and after implementation will be compared to determine whether stockouts have decreased by at least 30%.

### Inventory and Expiry Losses

Inventory holding costs and product expiry losses will be monitored and compared before and after adoption to determine whether the targeted 20–25% reduction has been achieved.

### Forecast Coverage

The system will be checked to ensure that weekly and monthly forecasts can be generated for the top 50–100 products.

### Chatbot Usability

Pilot users will provide feedback on the ease of using the chatbot to query stock and sales information.

### Dashboard Usability

Pilot users will provide feedback on whether the dashboard presents important information clearly and supports decision-making.

### System Performance

The system will be tested on modest hardware, such as a laptop or mid-range smartphone, to confirm that it remains operational under the intended conditions.

## 7. Conclusion

The business objectives and success criteria provide measurable targets for evaluating the success of Retail-X-AI. The project aims to reduce stockouts, lower inventory and expiry losses, improve demand forecasting, make retail information easier to access through an AI chatbot, and provide a simple dashboard for shop owners.

The defined success criteria will allow the project team and pilot users to determine whether the system delivers measurable business value and remains practical for small retail businesses.
